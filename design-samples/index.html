Logo

Badge gradient #f4bef4 → #bc51d6 → #8c34b2. The X is two filled diagonals with flat terminals (matching the wordmark), not round-capped strokes. Source of truth: src/app/icon.svg and src/components/phrasex-logo.tsx.

Buttons

One helper, actionButtonClass(state), drives all of them — idle (accent gradient), busy (accent-soft), muted (glass). Copy has a labelled and an icon-only variant; icon-only carries an aria-label.
